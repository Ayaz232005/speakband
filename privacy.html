Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SpeakBand ("we", "us") handles information when you use our free AI IELTS speaking-practice website at speakband-api.ayazawan273.workers.dev (the "Service"). We designed SpeakBand to need as little of your data as possible: there are no accounts, no tracking pixels, and no advertising.

2. At a glance

  • Your voice audio never reaches us. Transcription happens in your browser.
  • Transcripts are only sent to us when you ask for AI feedback, and only the text — never audio.
  • We store waitlist emails (only if you join) and an anonymous daily-use counter.
  • We never sell data, run ads, or share data for marketing.

3. Audio and transcripts

When you speak into the practice room, your browser's built-in speech recognition (the Web Speech API) transcribes your voice into text. In Chrome and Microsoft Edge, that transcription runs on the browser maker's servers — this is how those browsers work, and it is outside our control. Please check your browser maker's own privacy policy for how they handle it.

SpeakBand itself never receives, records, or stores your voice audio. What you see as a transcript in the practice room stays in your browser's memory unless you take the next step.

4. AI grading data

If you tap "Get AI band feedback", the transcript text (not audio) is sent to our servers and forwarded to our AI grading providers — currently Google Gemini and Groq — solely to produce your band scores and tips. Transcripts are processed ephemerally: we do not keep a copy after grading. Our providers process the text under their own terms; we do not send them your email, IP address, or any identifier.

5. Waitlist emails

If you join the waitlist, we store your email address in Cloudflare KV (our hosting provider's storage) so we can email you about launches such as PTE mode or the Pro tier. We email only about SpeakBand. You can ask to be removed at any time via the contact below, and we delete the address.

6. Automatic technical data

To enforce the free tier fairly (3 AI gradings per day per IP address), our servers see your IP address and keep an anonymous daily counter. We also see ordinary request metadata (browser type, timestamps) in server logs. We do not build profiles from this data.

7. How we use information

We use the limited data above only to: run the Service, enforce fair-use limits, send waitlist emails you asked for, and keep the Service secure. We do not sell personal data, do not share it for advertising, and do not use it to train models.

8. Subprocessors

Your data may pass through: your browser maker (speech transcription, when you speak), Google Gemini and Groq (AI grading of transcript text, only on request), and Cloudflare (hosting and storage). Each processes data under its own terms.

9. International transfers

Our providers operate servers in multiple countries, so your transcript text or email may be processed outside your country. We rely on our providers' safeguards for such transfers.

10. Retention

Transcripts: not retained after grading. Waitlist emails: kept until you unsubscribe or the waitlist closes. IP-based daily counters: expire automatically after 24 hours. Practice history you see in the app lives in your own browser's local storage — clearing your browser data removes it.

11. Cookies and local storage

We do not set tracking cookies. The practice app uses your browser's local storage only to keep your practice history and any waitlist signup queued on your device. No third-party analytics or ad trackers are present.

12. Security

We use HTTPS everywhere and keep data collection minimal by design, which is itself a safeguard. No internet service is perfectly secure, but the less we hold, the less there is to lose.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we hold so little (essentially just waitlist emails on request), most requests will be simple: contact us and we will act promptly, including deleting your waitlist email.

14. Children's privacy

SpeakBand is intended for learners aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if we learn we have, we delete it. If you are under 18, please use SpeakBand with a parent or guardian's knowledge.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for material changes, note it on the site. Continued use after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

16. Contact

Questions about privacy: message us on Instagram at @tryspeakband.

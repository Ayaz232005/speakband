Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. The short version

Every paid SpeakBand Pro subscription comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you're not happy for any reason, contact us within 14 days of your payment and we'll refund you in full — no questions asked, no forms to fill in.

2. The free tier is free forever

SpeakBand's free plan — 3 AI-graded speaking mocks per day with a free account — costs nothing and always will. There is nothing to pay and therefore nothing to refund on the free tier.

3. How Pro billing works

SpeakBand Pro is a monthly subscription. When you upgrade, you're billed once per month through our checkout provider, Lemon Squeezy. You can cancel at any time from your account page; cancellation stops future billing, and you keep Pro access until the end of the billing period you've already paid for.

4. How to ask for a refund

Message us on any of our official channels — Instagram, X, or YouTube, all @tryspeakband — and include the email address on your SpeakBand account plus the date of the payment. We'll confirm within 2 business days.

5. How refunds are paid

Refunds are processed through Lemon Squeezy back to your original payment method (card, PayPal, or whichever method you paid with). Depending on your bank or provider, the money typically arrives within 5–10 business days of the refund being issued.

6. Pre-launch honesty

As of the date above, SpeakBand Pro hasn't launched yet — the whole service is free while we build. This policy takes effect the day Pro billing goes live. Until then, there is nothing to pay and nothing to refund.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and note the change here. The version in force at the time of your payment is the one that applies to it.

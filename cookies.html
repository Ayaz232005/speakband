Cookie Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. The short version

SpeakBand sets exactly one cookie. No tracking cookies, no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies, no third-party cookies. That’s it.

2. The one cookie we set

  • Name: sb_session
  • Purpose: keeps you signed in to your free SpeakBand account.
  • Lifetime: 30 days from your last sign-in.
  • Flags: HttpOnly (JavaScript on the page can’t read it), Secure (sent over HTTPS only), SameSite=Lax.
  • When it’s set: only after you sign up or log in. Visitors who never create an account never get this cookie.

3. Local storage (not cookies, but worth knowing)

The practice app uses your browser’s local storage — data that stays on your device — to keep your practice history and to queue a waitlist signup if you’re offline. Nothing in local storage is sent to us except when you explicitly ask for AI feedback (transcript text only) or join the waitlist (your email).

4. Removing it

Log out from your account page and the sb_session cookie is deleted immediately. You can also clear it anytime in your browser settings (usually under Privacy → Cookies). Clearing local storage in the same place removes your on-device practice history.

5. Changes

If we ever need another cookie (for example, to remember a preference), we’ll list it here first. We don’t plan to.

6. Contact

Questions: message us on Instagram at @tryspeakband.

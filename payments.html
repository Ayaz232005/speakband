Payment Methods

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Pro hasn’t launched yet — nobody can pay us anything today, and the free tier needs no payment details at all. This page describes exactly how payments will work at launch, so there are no surprises.

1. Who handles your payment

SpeakBand Pro checkout runs through Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. That means your card details go to Lemon Squeezy’s secure, PCI-compliant checkout — they never touch SpeakBand’s servers. Lemon Squeezy also handles sales tax / VAT for your country automatically.

2. Accepted methods (at launch)

  • Visa — credit and debit
  • Mastercard — credit and debit
  • PayPal
  • Apple Pay

All payments are in USD. Your bank or card issuer may apply its own currency-conversion fee on top — that’s between you and them, and we can’t see or control it.

3. What you’re paying for

SpeakBand Pro — $9/month or $90/year — unlocks unlimited AI band gradings, full examiner reports, pronunciation scoring, and progress tracking. See Pricing for the full Free vs Pro breakdown.

4. Subscriptions and cancellation

Pro is a recurring subscription (monthly or yearly, your choice). Cancel anytime from your account page — you keep Pro until the end of the billing period you already paid for, then drop back to the free tier. Nothing is ever charged without your explicit checkout.

5. Refunds

Every Pro subscription starts with a 14-day money-back guarantee: not happy for any reason within 14 days of payment, and we refund you in full. Full details in our Refund Policy.

6. Security

Checkout pages are served over HTTPS with industry-standard encryption. We don’t store, see, or process card numbers — there is nothing for us to leak. If you ever see a SpeakBand payment page that doesn’t go through Lemon Squeezy, stop and tell us on Instagram at @tryspeakband — it isn’t us.

7. Questions

Message us on Instagram at @tryspeakband.

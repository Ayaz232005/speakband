Fair questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. Practice questions, timers and demo band reports are free with no account. Three AI gradings a day are free too. A paid tier later adds unlimited AI grading and progress tracking. The free tier stays free.

How is my speech processed?

Your browser’s built-in speech recognition transcribes your voice. In Chrome and Edge that runs on the browser maker’s servers; by default nothing is stored by us. Tapping “Get AI band feedback” sends only the transcript text to our servers for scoring; transcripts aren’t kept, only an anonymous daily-use counter.

Which tests does it cover?

IELTS Speaking Parts 1–3 today. PTE Academic speaking tasks are on the roadmap; join the waitlist below to vote on what comes first.

Do I need an account?

No — but a free account is better. Sign up free and your practice history and band scores live in your account, so you can track progress on any device. A Pro subscription ties to the same account when it launches. No sign-up needed to try your first mock: open the page, allow the mic, start speaking.

Will this replace a teacher?

No. It’s a practice multiplier. Ten focused minutes a day with instant feedback beats one weekly class for fluency, but a teacher is still best for personal strategy.

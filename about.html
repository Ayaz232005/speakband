About SpeakBand

The short version: speaking is the hardest IELTS skill to practise alone, and tutors are expensive. SpeakBand is a free AI speaking coach that tells you your band score before test day.

Why speaking is the problem

Reading, listening and writing can all be practised with a book and a timer. Speaking can't. You need someone to listen, someone who knows the band descriptors, someone who will tell you that your fluency is a 7 but your lexical resource is dragging you to a 6.5. A private tutor costs real money per hour — out of reach for most of the two million people who take IELTS every year.

SpeakBand exists to close that gap: real IELTS questions, real exam timings, and an AI examiner that scores you on the same four criteria a human examiner uses.

How it works

  • Pick a part. Part 1 interview, Part 2 cue card, Part 3 discussion, or the full 11-minute mock.
  • Speak out loud. Your browser transcribes your voice; the timers run exactly like the exam.
  • Get your band. Tap "Get AI band feedback" for an overall band plus four criterion scores, each with an examiner-style note and one concrete drill for your weakest area.

Three AI gradings a day are free, forever. No account needed to start.

Where we are (honestly)

SpeakBand is young and in active development. What's live today: the full practice room, real AI band scoring, and the free tier. What's coming: free accounts (so your history and scores follow you across devices), a Pro tier with unlimited grading and pronunciation scoring, and PTE Academic mode.

We'd rather tell you what's missing than pretend it's finished. If something's broken or confusing, tell us on Instagram — it genuinely shapes what gets built next.

Who's behind it

SpeakBand is an independent, zero-budget project by Ayaz Ahmad Awan, a student in Lahore, built with an AI assistant called Muse. No investors, no ads, no data selling — just a tool we wish existed when we needed it.
